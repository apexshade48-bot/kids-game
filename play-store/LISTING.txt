Word Stars

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Hear, say, and spell English words. Coins, stars, and a talking teacher.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Word Stars is a word game for families. Kids hear a word, say it, or type it, then earn coins and stars.

Learn in order:
Letters, Sounds, Beginner, Easy, Normal, Hard, Top, and Family talk.

Also in the game:
• Daily Talk — six everyday phrases
• Word of the day
• Spell, Quiz, and picture rounds
• Space — catch letters and say the word
• Shop clothes and a lucky spin
• Offline Teacher (llama3.2 on the family PC, when Ollama is running)
• Parent page — progress, likes, and a stop-play email

No ads. No in-app purchases required to start (Easy and starter levels are free).

The Android app opens your hosted Word Stars site (Trusted Web Activity). A parent or teacher should host the game at an HTTPS address.
