Privacy

Word Stars is a family word game. We keep data small and local to this app.

What we store

  • A player name and password you choose at sign up.
  • A parent email (for a play-review when the kid taps Stop).
  • Game progress: coins, stars, unlocks, clothes, likes, and chat on this server.
  • We do not ask for a phone number or home address.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no trackers, no sale of player data.
  • No location, contacts, or camera.
  • The microphone is used only when a player taps Say it, and only in the browser.

Chat

Players on the same game can send short messages. Do not share real names, addresses, or passwords in chat. A parent or admin can turn accounts off from the Admin panel.

Parents

This game is meant for a family or classroom. The owner account (Apex by default) can reset passwords, remove players, and manage coins.

Questions: ask the person who hosts this Word Stars server.
